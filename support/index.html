Getting help

Email us and you will get a reply from a person, usually within a couple of days.

tongfeiwu@hotmail.com

When something looks wrong with an analysis, it helps a lot if you tell us the camera angle you filmed from and roughly when you recorded it.

Common questions

  • Where should I put the phone? Front-diagonal, about 45° to your side, with your whole body in frame. That angle lets the app read the most.
  • Why does it say it cannot judge something? Some things genuinely cannot be seen from certain angles, or the clip was too dark or too short. The app tells you which instead of guessing.
  • How long should the clip be? Keep it under 30 seconds for now.
  • Why does it ask for my height and weight? Height lets it judge your punch path properly, and weight is what makes the calorie estimate meaningful. Both are optional.
  • How do I delete my data? Open Profile in the app. You can delete a single analysis, or your whole account.

Privacy

Your original video is never kept — it is analysed and deleted. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

常见问题

  • 手机放哪？斜前方约 45°，全身入画。这个角度能读到的最多。
  • 为什么有些项说判断不了？有些东西在某些角度确实看不出来，或者画面太暗、太短。App 会告诉你哪一项看不清，而不是硬猜一个结果。
  • 视频拍多长？目前请控制在 30 秒以内。
  • 为什么要填身高体重？身高用于判断出拳路径，体重是热量估算能成立的前提。两项都可以不填。
  • 怎么删除我的数据？在 App 里打开「我的」，可以删除单条评估，也可以删除整个账号。

隐私

你上传的原始视频从不保留——分析完即删除。完整说明见隐私政策。
