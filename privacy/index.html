拳有 / BoxMate

Privacy Policy

Effective 28 July 2026 · Last updated 28 July 2026

拳有 (BoxMate) is a boxing training app. You record a short clip of yourself, the app analyses your movement, and it gives you a coach-style breakdown and training sessions. This page explains exactly what happens to your data.

The short version.

Your original video is not kept. It is uploaded, analysed, and deleted immediately afterwards.

What we do keep is a short replay clip generated from your video with a skeleton drawn over it, plus the joint coordinates. People may still be recognisable in that replay clip — it is your own footage, darkened, with lines drawn on top.

You can delete any single analysis, or your whole account, from inside the app.

What we collect

AccountYour email address, or the private relay address Apple gives us if you use Sign in with Apple.
Training profileHeight, weight, stance (orthodox or southpaw), training goal, boxing experience, and arm span if you choose to enter it.
Video you recordUploaded for analysis only. Deleted as soon as the analysis finishes. Never stored in our database or file storage.
Analysis outputA short replay clip with a skeleton overlay, per-frame joint coordinates, and the categorised findings (for example how quickly your hand returns, how your shoulder turns).
Training recordsSessions you complete, check-ins, your weekly plan, and the feedback taps you give us (such as “accurate” / “not quite”).
Technical logsBasic server logs needed to run and debug the service.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no tracking of you across other apps or websites.
  • No contacts, no location, no microphone access.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

How your video is handled

When you submit a clip, it is sent to our analysis server, processed frame by frame to detect your body position, and then deleted. It is not written to our database and not written to file storage.

Two things are produced and kept:

  • A short replay clip. This is built from the frames of your own video, dimmed, with a skeleton drawn over the top. Frames where no body was detected are used as they are. This means faces, bodies and the room around you can still be recognisable.
  • Joint coordinates. A compressed file of numeric positions for each frame. On its own it is a set of numbers, not a picture.

Replay clips are served through time-limited private links. A link expires and cannot be reused later.

AI-generated coaching commentary

To write the coaching commentary you read in the app, we send the text of your analysis results to a third-party AI provider, together with your training goal, your experience level, and a few technical details about the clip — how long it was, the camera angle you chose, and the measurements taken from your movement. The provider returns written text.

We do not send your video, your replay clip, any images, your email address, your name, your height, your weight, or your stance. The provider receives the categorised findings and those few values in text form only.

Where your data is stored, and who processes it

SupabaseAccount authentication, database, and file storage. Our project is hosted in Singapore.
RailwayHosting for the analysis server.
AI providerGenerates the written coaching commentary, from text only (see above).
AppleSign in with Apple, App Store and TestFlight distribution.

These providers process data on our behalf so the app can work. If you are in a different country from where these services run, your data is transferred and stored on their infrastructure.

How long we keep things

  • Your original video: deleted immediately after analysis.
  • Replay clips, joint coordinates, analysis results, training records: kept until you delete them, or until you delete your account.
  • Deletion audit record: when an account is deleted we keep a one-line record containing a timestamp, a one-way hash fragment that cannot be turned back into your identity, and counts of what was removed. This exists so we can show a deletion actually happened. It contains no personal information.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete a single analysis. Removes that analysis, its replay clip and its coordinates.
  • Delete your account. In the app, open Profile and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your profile, every analysis, every replay clip and coordinate file, your training records and your weekly plan. It is not a deactivation and there is no recoverable copy.
  • Skip what you do not want to share. Height and weight make the analysis and the calorie estimate more accurate, but you can leave them out and still use the app.
  • Ask us anything. Write to the address at the bottom of this page and we will answer, including requests to access or correct your data.

If a subscription is ever added to the app, deleting your account will not by itself cancel it — subscriptions are managed in your Apple account settings.

Children

拳有 is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Access to your data requires your own signed-in session. Replay clips and coordinate files are kept in private storage and reached only through short-lived signed links. No system is perfect; if we ever discover a breach that affects you, we will tell you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will update the date at the top and, for anything significant, tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions, requests, or anything that looks wrong: tongfeiwu@hotmail.com

拳有 / BoxMate

隐私政策

2026 年 7 月 28 日生效 · 最后更新 2026 年 7 月 28 日

拳有是一款拳击训练 App。你拍一段自己的短视频，App 分析你的动作，给出教练视角的拆解和训练课。这一页说清你的数据到底经历了什么。

先说要紧的。

你上传的原始视频不保留——上传、分析、分析结束立刻删除。

保留的是一段由你的视频画面生成、叠加了骨骼的短回放动图，以及逐帧的关节坐标。那段回放里的人物可能仍可辨认——它就是你自己的画面，压暗后叠了线条。

你可以在 App 内删除任意单条评估，或删除整个账号。

我们收集什么

账号你的邮箱地址；若使用 Apple 登录，则是 Apple 提供的中转地址。
训练资料身高、体重、站架（正架或反架）、训练目标、拳龄，以及你选填的臂展。
你拍的视频仅用于分析。分析一结束即删除，从不写入我们的数据库或文件存储。
分析产出一段叠加骨骼的短回放动图、逐帧关节坐标，以及分档的观察结果（例如收手快慢、肩部转动情况）。
训练记录你完成的训练、打卡、本周计划，以及你点的反馈（例如「准」／「不太准」）。
技术日志运行和排错所必需的基础服务器日志。

我们不收集

  • 不使用广告标识符，不跨其他 App 或网站追踪你。
  • 不读取通讯录、不获取位置、不使用麦克风。
  • 不出售你的数据，不为广告目的共享你的数据。

你的视频是怎么处理的

你提交的视频会发送到我们的分析服务器，逐帧检测身体位置，然后被删除。它不写入数据库，也不写入文件存储。

产生并保留两样东西：

  • 一段短回放动图。它由你自己视频的画面生成，压暗后叠上骨骼线条；没有检测到人体的那些帧直接使用原画面。因此其中的人脸、身形和你所在的房间仍可能被辨认出来。
  • 关节坐标。一个压缩文件，记录每一帧各关节的数值位置。单独看是一组数字，不是画面。

回放动图通过限时私有链接提供，链接会过期，之后无法再次使用。

AI 生成的教练点评

为了生成你在 App 里读到的教练点评，我们会把分析结果的文字，连同你的训练目标、拳龄，以及这段视频的少量技术信息（时长、你选择的拍摄角度、从动作中测算出的数值），发送给第三方 AI 服务，由它返回文字内容。

我们不会发送你的视频、回放动图、任何图像、邮箱地址、姓名，也不会发送你的身高、体重和站架。对方收到的只是文字形式的分档结果与上述少量数值。

数据存在哪里，由谁处理

Supabase账号认证、数据库与文件存储。我们的项目位于新加坡。
Railway分析服务器的托管。
AI 服务商生成教练点评文字，仅接收文字（见上）。
AppleApple 登录、App Store 与 TestFlight 分发。

这些服务商代表我们处理数据，App 才能运行。如果你所在的国家与这些服务运行的地点不同，你的数据会被传输并存储在他们的基础设施上。

保留多久

  • 原始视频：分析结束即删除。
  • 回放动图、关节坐标、分析结果、训练记录：保留到你删除它们，或删除账号为止。
  • 删除审计记录：账号被删除时，我们保留一行记录，包含时间、一段无法反推回你身份的单向哈希片段，以及删除了哪几类数据的计数。它的作用是证明删除确实发生过，其中不含个人信息。

你的选择与权利

  • 删除单条评估。连同该条的回放动图与坐标文件一并删除。
  • 删除账号。在 App 里打开「我的」，选择「删除账号」。这会永久删除你的账号、资料、全部评估、全部回放动图与坐标文件、训练记录和周计划。这是真删除，不是停用，也没有可恢复的副本。
  • 不想给的可以不给。身高体重能让分析和热量估算更准，但不填也能正常使用 App。
  • 随时问我们。写信到本页底部的邮箱，包括查询或更正你的数据，我们都会回复。

若将来 App 加入订阅，删除账号本身不会取消订阅——订阅需在你的 Apple 账户设置中管理。

儿童

拳有不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会有意收集他们的数据。如果你认为有儿童注册了账号，请写信告诉我们，我们会删除。

安全

访问你的数据需要你自己已登录的会话。回放动图与坐标文件存放在私有存储中，只能通过限时签名链接访问。没有系统是完美的；如果我们发现了影响到你的数据泄露，会告知你。

本政策的变更

如果本政策发生影响到你的变更，我们会更新页首日期；重要变更会在 App 内告知你。
